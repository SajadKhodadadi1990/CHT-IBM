# Conjugate-Heat-Transfer---Immersed-Boundary-Base-Primary--Secondary-Grid-
OpenFOAM based heat transfer solver with primary–secondary mesh coupling for complex or moving internal boundaries. It uses a unified energy equation for fluid and solid regions and supports CHT ike thermal simulations with field mapping between meshes.


Sajad Khodadadi, Reza Maddahian, Xizhong Chen ,A coupled species transport, heat transfer and moving boundary approach for modeling deposition in turbulent flow
